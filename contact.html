The Rivers

Rimewild operates on three major coastal river systems in Lincoln and Lane counties. These rivers drain the Coast Range into the Pacific, running through old-growth timber and steep canyon terrain that most people never see.

Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How do I book a trip?
Use the contact form on this page, email us directly at will@supportwellnessglobal.com, or hit the "Book Now" button on any page. Your message goes to our guide, who will reply with availability and next steps. Nothing is booked until you confirm.
How far in advance should I book?
Winter steelhead season (December–April) books fastest, especially weekends. We recommend 2–4 weeks in advance for peak season. Midweek availability is usually better. Summer and fall trips are more flexible.
What's the cancellation policy?
Full refund with 72+ hours notice. Within 72 hours, we'll work with you to reschedule if possible. Weather cancellations by the guide are always fully refunded or rescheduled.
Do you offer gift certificates?
Yes. A guided trip on Oregon's Central Coast makes a great gift. Contact us with the trip type and recipient details, and we'll set it up. Gift certificates are valid for one year from purchase.
Can you accommodate large groups?
We can coordinate multi-boat trips for groups of 5 or more. Corporate outings, bachelor parties, and family reunions are welcome. Reach out with your group size and preferred dates for a custom quote.
