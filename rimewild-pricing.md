## Pricing Notes

- **Fishing** is priced premium ($395) vs market average (~$260). This is justified by the brand quality, all-inclusive gear, and Corey's local expertise. Could be adjusted down to $325-350 if needed to compete.
- **Hunting** is priced aggressively low vs market. This undercuts established outfitters significantly — may attract volume but could also signal lower quality. Consider raising to $3,500-4,000 for elk.
- **Family trips** at $350/group (not per person) is an excellent value proposition.
- **Bank & Wade** at $275 is right at market — good entry-level price point.
