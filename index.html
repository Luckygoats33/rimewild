The Waters

Rimewild Country

Central Oregon Coast rivers from the headwaters of the Coast Range to the tidal estuaries.

Siletz River

Steelhead • Chinook • Cutthroat

67 miles of Coast Range water. The Siletz is Rimewild's home river — winter steelhead runs from December through April make it one of the most productive fisheries on the Oregon coast.

Alsea River

Chinook • Steelhead • Cutthroat

Running from the Coast Range to Waldport, the Alsea delivers strong fall chinook runs and excellent bank access. A versatile river for all skill levels.

Siuslaw River

Chinook • Coho • Steelhead

The Siuslaw winds through dense coastal forest near Florence. Quieter than the Siletz, with fewer boats and more wildlife.

Yaquina System

Chinook • Steelhead • Bay Species

The Yaquina Bay and its tributaries near Newport offer unique tidal fishing opportunities alongside traditional river runs.

Why Rimewild

Local Water

Central Coast focus. These aren't rivers Corey drives four hours to reach. This is home water, fished in every condition, every season, every year since childhood.

Small Groups

Personal instruction and attention. You won't be one of six people in a boat. Rimewild keeps it small because that's how you actually learn.

Real Fishing

Meet Your Guide
Local knowledge isn't downloaded. It's earned one wet winter at a time.

Corey was born and raised on Oregon's Central Coast. He's been hunting and fishing these rivers and timber his entire life — not as a hobby picked up in adulthood, but as the thing that shaped how he sees this landscape.

He knows where steelhead hold after heavy rain on the Siletz because he's fished that water in every condition for decades. He knows where blacktail bed in the Coast Range because he's walked that timber since he was old enough to carry a rifle.

That kind of knowledge doesn't come from a guide school or a YouTube channel. It comes from a lifetime spent on the water and in the woods.

Before You Go

What to Bring

  • Layered clothing (rain is likely)
  • Quality rain jacket and pants
  • Warm hat and gloves (winter)
  • Polarized sunglasses
  • Lunch, snacks, and water
  • Camera
  • Valid Oregon fishing/hunting license

Oregon Licenses

An Oregon fishing or hunting license is required. Daily, annual, and non-resident licenses are available through ODFW. Licenses can be purchased at local sporting goods stores in Lincoln City and Newport.

Where to Stay

Lincoln City, Newport, Waldport, and Florence all offer lodging. We're happy to recommend places that work well for early-morning departures to the river.

FAQ

What if the weather is bad?
We fish in rain. Trips are only cancelled for dangerous conditions.

Catch-and-release?
Wild steelhead are released. Hatchery fish may be retained per ODFW regulations.

Cancellation policy?
Full refund with 72+ hours notice.
