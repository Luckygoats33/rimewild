Fall Chinook Report — Late September 2026

Water levels dropping into shape after last week's rain. The Siletz is running at 340 cfs, clear with a slight green tint. Chinook are pushing through the lower river in solid numbers — we're seeing fish from the Ojalla drift down to Moonshine County Park.
